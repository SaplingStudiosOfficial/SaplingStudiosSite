The Rusty Longsword

"The Rusty Longsword" is a distinctive fantasy adventure where I designed and implemented a unique gameplay system. Players explore rich 3D pixel art environments, collecting ingredients and engaging with characters throughout the day. The game features intricate "Where's Waldo"-style sections that challenge players to find hidden secrets.

At night, players take on the role of a bartender, crafting drinks that directly influence the story and interactions with characters. This dynamic bartending system is a core part of the game, as each drink choice impacts the unfolding narrative, adding depth and replayability. My role involved designing this system to ensure it seamlessly integrated with the overall experience.

Discover more about the game on its Steam page.

Features:

  • Unique Day/Night Cycle with Varied Gameplay
  • Detailed 3D Pixel Art Environments
  • Ingredient Collection and Character Interaction
  • Secret-Finding Challenges with Hidden Rewards
  • Dynamic Bartending System Affecting Story Outcomes
  • Replayable Narrative with Multiple Endings
