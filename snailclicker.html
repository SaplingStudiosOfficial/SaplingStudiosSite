About Snail Clicker

"Snail Clicker" is an engaging clicker game where your objective is simple: click the button to earn points. As you accumulate points, you can spend them to increase your point-gain rate and purchase auto-clickers to continue earning points even when you're not actively clicking.

Programmed using WebGL, this game challenges your strategy and time management as you decide when to click and when to invest in automation. It's the ultimate test of patience and precision—how fast can you grow your snail empire?

Try it out and see how far you can go!
