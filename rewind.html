About REWIND

REWIND is a temporal puzzle game where players reverse time to escape rooms they aren’t supposed to escape. The game introduces a unique mechanic called "Shadow," where players record their actions and transformations. Once they replay the recording, the actions and transformations are played back in reverse, allowing them to solve intricate puzzles designed around this mechanic.

Developed using Unreal Engine 5 and Event Graphs, "REWIND" leverages advanced visual scripting to create dynamic and engaging puzzle mechanics.
