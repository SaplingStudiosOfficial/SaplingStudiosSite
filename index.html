Biography

John Gonzalez has always been passionate about video games and storytelling. From a young age, he dreamed of creating worlds where players could embark on epic adventures and be the heroes of their own stories. This passion led him to pursue a career in game development, where he founded Sapling Studios.

With years of experience in game design, programming, and art, John has successfully developed and launched several critically acclaimed games. His work is characterized by a deep commitment to quality, creativity, and user experience.

  • Game Development: Unity, Unreal Engine, WebGL, GLSL
  • Design: UX/UI Design, Level Design, Puzzle Design, Narrative Design
  • Tools: Blender, Krita
  • Backend Development: Node.js, API Integration, Server Development
  • Project Management: Agile Methodologies, Team Leadership
