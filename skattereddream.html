Skattered Dream

Skattered Dream is a platformer where you traverse through engaging levels and unlock unique hats each time you complete a level. These hats can then be found and equipped in the main menu, adding a fun and customizable aspect to the game.

Developed using C# and Unity, "Skattered Dream" combines smooth platforming mechanics with creative customization options to provide an immersive gaming experience.
